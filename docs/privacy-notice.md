# Draft Privacy Notice

> Draft for church and Texas legal review. This repository does not assert legal compliance.

## In-product initial disclosure

### How Gifts in Service uses your information

Your approved Gifts in Service profile may be viewed by authorized church staff and designated ministry leaders for the purpose of identifying and contacting potential volunteers. Please do not include information you would not be comfortable sharing with those authorized users.

An artificial intelligence (AI) assistant—a computer program that writes replies based on patterns—will help you describe skills, experience, hobbies, and interests you may be willing to share. Your unfinished questions and answers are saved in protected form for up to 30 days so you can return. They are deleted when you approve your profile or when the 30 days end. Only the exact profile you review and approve is available to authorized staff or used in volunteer search.

If an answer raises a concrete concern about a volunteer activity or placement, the service may require neutral profile wording telling staff to discuss the concern and the role's objective safety requirements before placement. The wording will not state a diagnosis or private reason, and you will see it before deciding whether to approve the profile.

Anyone who can use a verified email address associated with a profile—including anyone with access to a shared mailbox—may be able to open that profile and its unfinished conversation.

Submitting a profile does not commit you to accept any request to serve. Skills and qualifications are self-reported unless the church separately verifies them.

Gifts in Service is for adults age 18 or older.

## Full draft notice

Gifts in Service helps church staff and ministry leaders with permission identify adults who may be willing to share their experience, practical abilities, hobbies, or interests. The information comes from the person who creates the profile. Gifts in Service is not a public directory. It does not assign volunteers, verify licenses or qualifications, perform background checks, or hold pastoral-care records.

Before a profile is created or updated, an AI assistant helps the person describe what they may be willing to share and what they would rather not include. The service temporarily saves the unfinished questions and answers, any proposal shown inside the resumable conversation, a simple progress estimate, and short notes that help the assistant remember what the person has already said or chosen to skip. The information is encrypted, which means it is scrambled so people or systems without the right digital key cannot read it.

The unfinished conversation is kept for no more than 30 days so the person can return without starting over. If the service recognizes a health disclosure, it does not keep that response; it keeps only a generic marker that a turn was omitted. If an answer separately raises a concrete concern about a supported volunteer activity or placement, the service keeps only the affected role category—not the diagnosis, evidence, relationship, or private reason—and requires neutral profile wording telling staff to discuss a potential role-safety concern and the role's safeguards before placement. Supported areas include care and supervision, driving, finances, pastoral relationships and visits, food, first aid, facilities, security/emergency response, confidential systems, and general placement. The person sees that wording before approval, but cannot save a profile that removes the required caution while retaining the pending interview. A separate draft opened on the final-review page after selecting **Create a draft** stays only in the current browser tab and is lost if it is reloaded or closed. Anyone who can use a verified email address associated with the profile can reach the saved unfinished conversation. If the address is a shared mailbox, anyone with access to that mailbox may be able to open it. Staff cannot search the unfinished conversation or the role category. The application does not put either into site-use tracking, logs, or error reports. Pending state is deleted from the working service when the profile is approved, when the person deletes the profile, or when the 30 days end; after approval, staff see only the exact profile the person approved.

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the outside technology company that runs the secure computers, database, email, sign-in, and AI tools used by Gifts in Service. It processes the current conversation, an approved profile, or a staff search only when needed to provide that feature. Before launch, the church must confirm that the AI provider will not keep conversations or record their contents in the provider's service logs.

The person reviews the entire profile before approval. The service then keeps the exact approved text, a computer-generated search aid made only from that text, the person's display name and verified email addresses, dates showing agreement and profile status, whether email was delivered, and limited records needed for sign-in, security, and accountability. Skills and qualifications are based on what the person reports unless the church verifies them separately.

Authorized users may use profiles only to identify and contact possible volunteers. A profile does not commit anyone to accept a request. Staff should independently confirm current availability, licenses, screening, safety requirements, and professional scope.

Members can view and update the exact profile, change their display name, manage verified email addresses, pause or restart the profile, confirm that it is still correct, or permanently delete it. Deletion removes the profile, contact information, unfinished conversation, and access to that profile from signed-in devices and sign-in links. A mailbox-wide sign-in link may still open another profile associated with the same address or start a new profile, but it cannot reopen the deleted profile. A small security record may remain, but it does not contain the person's name, email, or profile. Protected backup copies may contain deleted information for up to 35 more days. Those copies are not available through the normal service and are removed as old backups expire. If a backup must be restored after a serious failure, later deletion records are applied before the restored information can be used.

The service asks once a year whether a profile is still correct and sends two follow-up reminders. If the person does not respond, the profile is hidden from search after 58 weeks and permanently deleted after 62 weeks. Limited security, email-delivery, profile-status, and accountability records may remain where needed to operate and protect the service. The exact text of staff searches is deleted after 90 days.

Gifts in Service is for adults age 18 or older. It asks only for adult confirmation, not a birth date or exact age. People should not enter passwords, identification or account numbers, addresses, diagnoses, pastoral or family matters, background-check details, or information about others.

AWS runs the online service and its AI tools as described above. GitHub processes technical release information for the people who maintain the service. The application does not include outside site-use tracking, advertising, invisible tracking images, remotely loaded fonts, or a public list of profiles.

Questions, corrections, deletion help, or concerns should go to the privacy email address displayed in the application. The church must replace the example name and contact information and approve this notice before using the service with real member information.
